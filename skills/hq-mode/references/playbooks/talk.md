# Talk

**You own a deck the audience can follow at speaking speed.**

1. Load the `lab-presentation` skill and follow its workflow: the audience brief, the text
   outline, the slides, the check, and the speaker notes when wanted. The slide rules, the
   build and the image recipe are in its reference pages; this page repeats none of them.
2. Read the source text and any exemplar: a peer's deck, a template. Follow its structure and
   register, never its wording.
3. Recorded talks: per-slide recordings, short gaps, PowerPoint-exported frames. Bad audio →
   ask for a re-record; never denoise.

**Reply:** the deck path, slides changed, preview images checked, and open choices.
